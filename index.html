Ideas to investigate in Advanced Mode

Change α: With the alternative effect fixed, decrease α from .05 to .01. What happens to the red Type I error area? What happens to the green Type II error area and power?

Change the tail: With δ positive, compare right-tailed and two-tailed tests at the same α. Which has greater power for this particular positive effect? What happens if δ becomes negative?

Change the effect: Move the alternative distribution farther from or closer to the null. How does the overlap change? What happens to β and power?
