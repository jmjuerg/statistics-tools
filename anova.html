3. Interpret the F-distribution

The F-distribution describes which F-statistics are expected if the population means are equal.

The shaded right-tail area is the p-value. Because F is nonnegative, the ANOVA F-test rejects for sufficiently large values of F. Changing the significance level changes the rejection cutoff, not the observed statistic or its p-value.
